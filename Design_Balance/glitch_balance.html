Arts

Digital Art Lab

數位藝術實驗場

  • Explore interactive installations blending code, light and motion.
  • Hands-on workshops in generative art, AR/VR and creative coding.
  • A testing ground where emerging artists experiment with new media.
Contemporary Art Exhibition

當代藝術展演場

  • Rotating exhibitions featuring leading contemporary artists.
  • Live performances, talks and curated gallery tours.
  • A dynamic space bridging visual art with performance culture.
Asia Comics Warehouse

亞洲漫畫倉庫

  • An extensive archive of manga, manhua and manhwa from across Asia.
  • Original artwork, sketches and rare editions on display.
  • Celebrating the artists shaping Asia's comic culture.
